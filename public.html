Why Conservation Trips

Your prime tour & travel partner in Uganda

We are a fully-fledged travel, tourism and conservation management company, proudly local and deeply knowledgeable, serving both regional and global clients with a dedication to excellence, safety and conservation.

  • Gorilla trekking experts

    Specialist permit procurement, guides with decade-long Bwindi experience, and group sizes managed for authentic encounters.

  • Conservation-first

    Every trip contributes directly to Uganda's conservation efforts and local community livelihoods. Travel that gives back.

  • Custom itineraries

    Tailored to your timeline, travel style, group size and budget. No two trips are the same.

  • Safari vehicles built for it

    4x4s with pop-up roofs, USB charging, cooler boxes and first-aid kits, made for comfort and wildlife viewing.

  • Licensed & insured

    Registered with Uganda Tourism Board and Uganda Wildlife Authority, with rigorous safety protocols throughout.

  • 24/7 support

    A dedicated trip advisor is available from your first enquiry through to your safe return home.

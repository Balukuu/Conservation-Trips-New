Cross the Equator

Stand on the Equator, One Foot in Each Hemisphere

The Equator runs straight through Uganda, and it is easy to include on a safari. Two monuments make natural stops on the road to the western parks, and we build the time for photographs into your route.

Kayabwe
The white ring on the Kampala–Masaka road: the classic stop on the way to Lake Mburo, Lake Bunyonyi and Bwindi.
Kikorongo
The mirror-steel globe beside Queen Elizabeth National Park, on the road to Kasese, the Rwenzoris and the Kazinga Channel.
